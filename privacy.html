VCViceCityClips Scheduler

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 10, 2026

ViceCityClips Scheduler is a personal tool used only by the creator behind the ViceCityClips accounts. This page explains what it stores and why.

What it stores

  • Sign-in tokens for the creator's own connected TikTok, YouTube and Instagram accounts, so the tool can publish at the scheduled time.
  • Basic profile details those platforms return: account ID, display name, username and profile picture address, shown in the tool so the creator can see which account a post goes to.
  • From TikTok, the creator info TikTok provides before each post (allowed privacy choices, whether comments, duets and stitches are switched off, and the longest allowed video), and the status of each post.
  • The creator's own videos, captions, posting times and per-post choices.

Where it is stored

Everything is stored only on the creator's own computer. Nothing is stored on this website.

How it is used

The data is used only to publish the creator's videos to the creator's own accounts and to show their status. It is never sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising or profiling. The tool does not collect data about other people, such as followers or viewers.

Removing data and access

Disconnecting an account in the tool deletes its stored token. Deleting a clip deletes its file and details. Access can also be revoked at any time in TikTok, Google or Instagram account settings.

Contact
