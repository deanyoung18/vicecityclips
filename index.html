VCViceCityClips Scheduler

Schedule short gaming clips to TikTok, YouTube Shorts and Instagram Reels

ViceCityClips Scheduler is a personal tool used by the creator behind the ViceCityClips accounts. The creator uploads their own short gaming clips once, reviews each post, and picks a time. At that time the tool publishes the clip to the creator's own TikTok, YouTube and Instagram accounts.

How it works

  1. Connect accounts. The creator signs in to their own TikTok, YouTube and Instagram accounts through each platform's official login (TikTok Login Kit, Google OAuth, Instagram Login).
  2. Upload clips. The creator adds their own videos and writes a caption and hashtags for each one.
  3. Review each TikTok post. For every clip, the creator sees which TikTok account it will post to, chooses who can watch it (there is no default), chooses whether to allow comments, duets and stitches (all off until turned on), discloses branded or promotional content when it applies, and agrees to TikTok's Music Usage Confirmation.
  4. Schedule. The creator picks a time. The clip is published only after this review and consent, using the TikTok Content Posting API, the YouTube Data API and the Instagram Graph API.
  5. Track status. The tool shows whether each post is scheduled, publishing, posted or failed, and tells the creator that a TikTok post can take a few minutes to appear on their profile.

What it does not do

  • It never adds watermarks, logos or promotional text to videos.
  • It never posts anything the creator did not upload, review and schedule.
  • It never reads, collects or stores other people's data, and never sells or shares any data.
  • It is not offered to the public. Only the creator's own accounts are connected.
